Write ban={"CardName1","CardName2"} in weapons/ranks
-> these cards are banned if the weapon/rank is selected

For animated card art:

Put each frame of animation in the slots after the card in card spritesheet,
and add animated = <number of frames per loop> to the card in script.lua

These frames will be played on the card in a loop.

To control the speed of the animation, add ani_spd = <slowdown rate> to the card (default 1)
This is effectively equal to making ani_spd copies of each frame in card spritesheet

To make the animation play only when your mouse is over it, add ov_play = true (default false)
